    How this was read

    The drawing was traced from the 600 dpi scan. Terminal numbers on the junction blocks match the breaker numbers, so each circuit is followed through the looms by its number. The drawing does not mark polarity, so it is inferred from the − terminals, the earth symbols and the battery markings. The main switch sits on the battery negatives (confirmed on the boat). Radios and the heater bypass it through the permanent negative and their own fuses. Short diagonal stubs into terminal blocks are not redrawn; switch to “Over scan” to see them.
