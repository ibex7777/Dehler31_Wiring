# Dehler 31 wiring diagram

An interactive, English redraw of the factory electrical wiring diagram for the Dehler 31 (sheet 14A, "Dehler 34/31").

Pick a circuit, or tap any wire, light or component, and its whole path lights up:

- **red**: positive
- **black**: negative
- **blue**: looms where positive and negative run together

You can view the clean redraw, the redraw over the original scan, or the scan on its own. The German labels are translated, and the original terms are listed on the page.

## How it was made

The wires were traced from a 600 dpi scan of the factory drawing. The terminal numbers on the junction blocks match the breaker numbers on the switch panel, so each circuit is followed through the looms by its number. The drawing doesn't mark polarity, so positive and negative are worked out from the − terminals, the earth symbols and the battery markings.

On this drawing the main switch isolates the battery **negatives** (confirmed on the boat). The heater and the radios bypass it through their own fuses and the permanent negative ("Dauer-Minus").

## Caveats

This is the factory drawing, not any particular boat. Boats of this age have usually been modified. A few connections in the drawing are ambiguous, and the page lists them under "Check on the boat". Check your own boat before relying on it.

## Files

`index.html` is self-contained: the drawing data, the script and the scan are all embedded. Only the fonts load from Google Fonts, and the page still works without them. It's served with GitHub Pages.
